Teaching

Courses I have designed and taught as instructor of record.

Course Code Course Title & Summary
ECON 320 Intermediate Microeconomic Analysis

A calculus-based treatment of the full arc of microeconomic theory: consumer preferences and choice, producer optimization, and market structures from perfect competition through monopoly and oligopoly. The course extends to game theory, uncertainty and asymmetric information, and market failures such as externalities and public goods — taught at full rigor and without dilution, and designed so that students of varied preparation, from transfer students to mathematics majors, can reach genuine command of the material.

ECON 402 Labor Economics

A study of how labor markets function and how policy shapes them, spanning labor demand and supply, human capital, compensating differentials, unions, and unemployment. The course pays particular attention to pay and productivity and to questions of gender, race, and ethnicity in the labor market, and it moves students from modeling these markets to analyzing the real policy questions each raises.

ECON 440 Economics of Income Inequality & Discrimination

A study of the economics of distribution, from the measurement of inequality to the theory of optimal taxation. The course begins with the normative question — what a just distribution of opportunity would be, in Rawlsian, Dworkinian, and Senian terms — before turning to economic theory and evidence, including the current debate over a wealth tax.

ECON 438 Education Economics

An upper-level course on how education is produced, financed, and returned to, taught largely from original empirical research on school policy. Students learn to assess causal evidence — from class-size and school-finance studies to school choice — and to weigh what the evidence can and cannot establish about policy.

*Note for currently enrolled students: Comprehensive course schedules, weekly readings, and grading rubrics are securely hosted inside the university Learning Management System (LMS).
